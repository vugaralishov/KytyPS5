The toolbar icons refresh.svg, global-settings.svg, input-mapping.svg,
edit-configuration.svg, remove-configuration.svg and trophy.svg are from Lucide
(https://lucide.dev): refresh-cw, sliders-horizontal, gamepad-2, square-pen,
trash-2 and trophy. Only the stroke color was changed to #000000; the launcher
recolors them at runtime.
